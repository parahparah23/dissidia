## What's new

### Lighting (Ray-Traced Shadows on)
- **Attacks light up the stage.** Glowing attack effects now cast light in their own colour, scaled to the size of the effect: big attacks light the floor and fighters around them, small sparks and dodges stay faint. Tune it in Settings > Debug > Effect Light Brightness / Reach.
- **Stage lights.** Pandaemonium, Crystal World, Empyreal Paradox and five more stages have hand-placed light sources with their own shadows, so they are no longer lit only by the sky.
- **Glowing crystals.** The breakable crystals of Crystal World and Empyreal Paradox give off light until they break.
- **EX Cores glow** with a soft blue light.

### Reflections
- **Only shiny surfaces reflect.** Water, ice, crystal, marble and polished floors reflect; grass and dirt no longer do, and walls and pillars can.
- **Ray-traced reflections look better:** no more black see-through spires, High is sharp instead of blurry, the whole sky is reflected, and spell effects (Watera, Thundaga, beams) show up in reflections. Medium is also faster (about +5 fps on a Retroid Pocket 6).

### Menus and screens
- **Settings from inside the game:** a Settings entry on the title screen, the start menu and the battle pause menu.
- **Tall and folding-phone screens:** the picture fills the screen, the HUD moves to the edges, and the touch controls get their own layout. Settings > Display > Tall Screen.

### Fixes
- The game no longer crashes when you quit.
- Lines across walls and floors, and striped floor reflections, with SSAO / ray-traced shadows on Adreno GPUs.
- The settings screen's opening animation works on Vulkan.
