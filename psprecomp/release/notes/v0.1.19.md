## Highlights

- **Online play rebuilt.** Online fights run on new netcode: the full analog stick works, input delay is steady, and one lost packet no longer freezes the fight. Players can find a direct path to each other through the party server (fewer hops, lower ping), and Group Battle rooms work again. Online fights above 30 fps no longer end by themselves after about 10 seconds.
- **Button prompts that match your controller.** The game's button symbols can be drawn as Xbox, PlayStation or Nintendo buttons, or as your keyboard's keys. Settings > Controls > Button Prompts (Automatic follows what you play with).
- **Full analog movement.** The left stick moves you in any direction, not only eight, in battle and on the world map. On the world map the camera no longer swings around while you walk; turn it with the right stick.
- **New save manager.** Settings > Saves: import saves from a folder or a .zip / .7z / .rar (US, EU and JP saves all work), export them, and restore backups. Every save that gets replaced is backed up first, so a cloud sync or import can be undone.
- **New Android setup app.** A native Android app with a new look: portrait or landscape, touch or controller, a live build log, building in the background with a notification, and log sharing when something goes wrong.

## Online
- **Show Ping** (Settings > Network, off by default): your ping to the other players in the top right corner.
- **Connection warnings:** the game now tells you what went wrong: the server is not answering, a player dropped out, high ping, packet loss, or why a fight ended.
- **UPnP Port Forward** (Settings > Network) for direct play without the party server: asks your router to open the game's port and shows the public address to give your friends.
- **Direct connections:** in Internet mode, two players in a room now try to connect to each other directly and only fall back to the party server if that fails (for example behind strict or carrier-grade NAT).
- Players above 30 fps keep their own frame rate online instead of dropping to the slowest player's.
- Watched fights in Group Battle use the new netcode too.
- Players on older versions still connect; their fights use the old netcode.

## Controls
- **L / R on the triggers** instead of the shoulder buttons (Settings > Controls).
- **Player 1 controller:** choose which connected controller plays.
- **Keyboard:** one key for each two-button move: dodge (D), dash (F), EX Mode (E), summon (V), assists (1 and 2).
- The Controls page is now shown on every device, not only on touch screens.
- **Paste** on the on-screen keyboards (a Paste key, or Ctrl+V), handy for server addresses and sync folders.

## Saves
- Export all saves to a folder or as a single .zip.
- Saves are also backed up once a day; restore any backup from Settings > Saves.
- Duplicate a save into another slot, move it, rename it on the save list, or delete it (deleted saves can be restored from the backups).
- Cloud sync conflicts are clearer: both versions side by side with the device, save time and play time of each, and "Keep the newer save of each" settles them all at once.

## Setup
- The installers check the Dissidia 1 disc and the undub disc as soon as you pick them and tell you what is wrong, instead of failing later.
- When an install fails, the PC setup offers buttons to open setup.log or show it in its folder; the Android app can share or save the build log and offers a crash report.
- The Android setup app works for secondary users on a phone (not only the owner).

## Fixes
- Black screen when starting a Story Mode chapter ("Depart on a journey") above 30 fps.
- Online fights ending by themselves about 10 seconds in above 30 fps.
- Group Battle rooms failing with "Room has been deleted due to a connection error".
- With Japanese voices, six story movies showed the undub disc's picture instead of the English one.
- Windows: adding Bartz's Dissidia 1 moves stopped with an error, and "Try again" afterwards did not work.
- Linux: HDR was greyed out as "Unavailable" on Wayland desktops (GNOME, KDE).
