## Highlights

- **Install your own texture packs.** Settings > Graphics > **Install a Texture Pack** opens your file picker: choose a texture pack you downloaded as a .zip (on a PC also .7z / .rar with 7-Zip installed). PPSSPP-style packs for Dissidia 012 work: the folder with the pack's textures.ini is found anywhere in the archive. The PC and Android setup can do the same: **Add your own texture pack**.
- **Several texture packs, switch between them.** Every pack you install stays installed. Settings > Graphics > **Texture Pack** picks the one to use (or Off); a pack you just installed is used from the next start. **Delete a Texture Pack** removes the ones you no longer want.
- **Get our HD textures from the game.** If the Dissidia Recomp HD pack is not installed, Settings > Graphics has **Download Dissidia Recomp HD**: the game downloads and installs it (about 1.3 GB; WiFi is best).
- **Android: textures no longer show up in your gallery.** The game's texture files were listed as photos in the gallery and file managers; they are hidden now (open the setup app once to clean up an existing install).

## Texture packs
- The setup's HD textures are the Dissidia Recomp HD pack: the D012FFRT community pack by Niosa plus our own AI upscales of the other textures.
- A pack you installed with an earlier version stays installed and appears in the Texture Pack list.
- A texture pack file is checked as soon as you pick it: a .zip without a textures.ini is refused with a message, and a broken file never replaces a pack you already have.
- On Android, packs are kept once (in the game's folder), so a pack you delete in the game does not come back.
