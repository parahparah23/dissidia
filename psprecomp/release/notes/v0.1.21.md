## Highlights

- **ISO patch mods, starting with Emendatus.** Install a disc patch (.xdelta) from Settings > Mods > Install ISO Patch, with no second ISO and no rebuild. Emendatus works: its new character Aerith is selectable and fully playable, and its changes to the other characters' moves apply. Mods can be turned on and off, or uninstalled, from the same page.
- **Character Shadows** (Settings > Effects > Ray Tracing, next to Ray-Traced Shadows): Off, Soft (default) or Sharp. Fighters no longer get blocky shadows from their own hair and body with ray-traced shadows on.
- **Field of View slider** (Settings > Display): -20 to +40 degrees from the game's own. In battle, lock-on and the target ring follow the new view.
- **Borderless window mode** (Settings > Display): Display now cycles Window, Borderless, Fullscreen.

## Mods
- Settings > Mods warns when two mods change the same files or use the same character slot.
- Turning a mod off now really turns it off, and saves keep their abilities so the mod can be turned back on later.

## Graphics
- Character Shadows: **Off** keeps ray-traced and contact shadows off the fighters, **Soft** lets stage shadows fall on them and their own body shade them lightly, **Sharp** shades them exactly like the stage.
- Stage lights, EX Core lights and attack lights now also skip a fighter's own body and light fighters smoothly.
- Button prompts keep your controller style (Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, keyboard) with an HD texture pack installed. With the PSP style, the pack's own prompts are shown as before.

## Fixes
- Stage lights, EX Core lights and attack lights were missing when reflections were turned off.
- Random picks such as "random opponent" were the same every time after loading a save; they now differ between runs.
- Android setup app: the build stopped at "Adding Bartz's Dissidia 1 moves".
- Windows: unpacking the .zip by hand right after a release could make the game build fail ("build.ninja still dirty") in time zones west of UTC.
- The crystal on the Android setup app's progress page now sits inside its orbiting ring.
