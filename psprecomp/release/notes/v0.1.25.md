## Highlights

- **Tag Team Battle (EXPERIMENTAL).** A new mode under Battle Mode and Communications Mode: Budokai Tenkaichi style tag battles with two or more fighters per side on the stage at once, partners you can call in instead of an assist, call-ins when a fighter falls, and its own HUD. This mode is a work in progress: expect bugs, odd camera moments and missing pieces. Summonstones are turned off in tag battles for now. Plain Quick Battle, Group Battle and the story modes are unchanged.
  - **Mode Select:** Team 2 vs 2, Team 3 vs 3, 1 vs 1 Duel, Tag Duel (one fighter each, your next member tags in) and Free-for-all (up to 4 players).
  - **Battle Lobby:** stage, rules, judgment, EX Burst and CPU fill (CPU fighters of a strength you choose for empty places), in single player and online.
  - **Single player:** put CPU fighters on your team (CPU rows with their own strength / level / type), choose Assist: Default (normal assists) or Partner (your tag partner comes in).
  - **Online (Communications > Tag Team Battle):** the host's mode and rules apply to everyone; up to 10 players in a room; two players against a CPU party (PvE).
- **Test Controls.** Settings > Controls > **Test Controls** shows a PSP that lights up the buttons and stick you press, plus a Controller view with both sticks, L2 / R2, L3 / R3 and every pad button - one tab per player.
- **Easier R / L moves.** Pressing R (or L) and a face button a moment apart now counts as the combination (dodge, dash, EX Mode, summon, assists): Settings > Controls > **Button Chord Window**. Mashing during an EX Burst is not slowed down by it.
- **The battle music no longer stops in long fights.** Battle tracks the game plays only once now loop.

## Settings
- **Keyboard Controls** (Controls page): a page listing every keyboard key, including the keys for two-button moves.
- **L / R Buttons** is a Shoulders / Triggers choice instead of an on/off switch.
- Settings > Debug: the Jecht combo timing helper is back (under FX Shaders), game debug rows are no longer limited in number, tag team rows sit in their own submenu, and **Reset All** puts every debug setting back to its default.

## Fixes
- More memory for characters, effects and sound in big battles (the PSP's extra high RAM is used).
